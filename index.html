About Venice Beach
Venice Beach is part boardwalk theater, part skate mythology, part sunset postcard, and part beautifully overconfident Los Angeles daydream. The idea here is simple: move through real places, unlock actual landmarks, and let the route feel a little cinematic instead of pretending exercise has to be dull.
